# Human Outside the Lines

This project is a personal website that explores stories, identity, and thoughts beyond conventional boundaries.  
It reflects the voice of an "out-of-the-box human" — someone who embraces complexity, honesty, and creativity outside of social norms.  

The site includes sections such as:
- **Stories**: Narratives that capture real experiences and perspectives.  
- **Thoughts**: Reflections and ideas that challenge ordinary ways of thinking.  
- **Who Am I**: A space to express identity and authenticity.  

The purpose of this project is to share human depth and individuality, showing that life is not confined to a single line or definition.
